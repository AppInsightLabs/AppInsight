What AppInsight does

AppInsight shows which installed apps are able to use your microphone, camera, and location, and records when Android reports that an app used them. It displays what Android reports. When Android does not identify the app behind an event, AppInsight shows the event without naming an app.

Optional Shizuku or root mode gives AppInsight access to deeper reporting from Android. Enabling it is your choice.

AppInsight is a transparency tool. It shows activity on your device; it does not block apps, remove apps, or change other apps' permissions.

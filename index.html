questions

Straight answers

Is this a permission manager?

No. Android already lets you grant and revoke permissions. AppInsight shows you what apps actually did with the permissions they hold, which is the part Android buries.

Do I need root?

No. It works out of the box. If you want every event named, system apps included, Shizuku gets you there without root.

What is Shizuku?

A free, open source tool that lets apps like AppInsight see deeper into Android. You set it up once, no root and no computer needed, and AppInsight walks you through every step.

Why should I trust this?

Don't trust it, check it. AppInsight ships without internet permission, which means Android will not let it send anything anywhere. That is not a promise we're making, it is how the app is built.
